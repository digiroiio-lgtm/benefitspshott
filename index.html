Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about ED treatments, the P-Shot and treatment in Turkey

The P-Shot (Priapus Shot) is a regenerative medical treatment that uses platelet-rich plasma (PRP) derived from your own blood to stimulate tissue repair and new blood vessel growth in the penis. A small blood sample is drawn, spun in a centrifuge to concentrate the growth factors, and the resulting PRP is injected into specific areas of the penile tissue. These growth factors trigger angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation), collagen production and neural regeneration, resulting in improved erectile function, enhanced sensitivity and in many cases a modest increase in girth.

A topical numbing cream is applied to the treatment area 20-30 minutes before the injections, making the procedure well-tolerated by the vast majority of patients. Most men describe the sensation as a mild pressure or brief sting rather than significant pain. The entire procedure takes approximately 30-45 minutes and you can return to normal activities the same day.

Shockwave therapy is entirely non-invasive — no needles or injections are involved. A probe is applied to the exterior of the penis and delivers low-intensity acoustic waves that penetrate the tissue, stimulating blood vessel formation and activating dormant stem cells. The P-Shot involves injections but uses your own biological material (PRP). Both treatments address the vascular root cause of ED rather than just masking symptoms. Many patients benefit from combining both therapies.

Most men with mild to moderate erectile dysfunction are good candidates for the P-Shot and/or shockwave therapy. These treatments are particularly effective for vascular ED (the most common type), men who have not achieved satisfactory results with oral medications like Viagra or Cialis, and those who prefer not to rely on medication long-term. Men with severe neurogenic ED or certain systemic conditions may require different approaches. A full medical consultation will determine the most appropriate treatment plan for your specific situation.

Savings typically range from 60-70% compared to UK prices and 65-75% compared to US prices. For example, a P-Shot costing £2,500-£3,500 in the UK costs £800-£1,500 in Turkey. A combined P-Shot and shockwave therapy package costing £4,500-£6,000 in the UK is available from £1,500-£2,800 in Turkey, all-inclusive. When you factor in accommodation and travel (often totalling £400-£800 from the UK), the savings are still exceptional.

Whether in the UK, US or Turkey, look for clinics staffed by board-certified urologists or andrologists with specific expertise in men's sexual health. In Turkey, JCI accreditation indicates internationally recognised quality standards. Ask about the doctor's specific experience with P-Shot or shockwave procedures, the equipment used, the consultation process, what aftercare is provided, and what happens if you experience complications. Transparent pricing without hidden costs, proper medical screening before treatment and a personalised approach rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol are all positive indicators.
