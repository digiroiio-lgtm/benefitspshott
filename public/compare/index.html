Detailed Pros & Cons by Treatment

Oral Medications (PDE5 Inhibitors)

✓ Pros

  • Extremely effective for mild-moderate ED
  • Non-invasive, simple to use
  • Well-established safety profile
  • Widely available, no special equipment

✗ Cons

  • Temporary effects only (4-36 hours)
  • Requires planning before sexual activity
  • Ongoing medication costs
  • Potential side effects (headache, flushing)

P-Shot (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

✓ Pros

  • Targets root cause of vascular ED
  • Results last 12-18 months or longer
  • Spontaneous erections after results appear
  • Single or dual treatment option

✗ Cons

  • Requires specialist clinic access
  • Higher upfront cost (£2,500-£3,500 UK)
  • Results take 4-8 weeks to manifest
  • Requires booster treatments after 18 months

Shockwave Therapy (Li-ESWT)

✓ Pros

  • Regenerates blood vessels (neovascularisation)
  • Drug-free, non-invasive approach
  • 60-75% effectiveness rate in trials
  • Results improve over 3 months post-treatment

✗ Cons

  • Requires multiple sessions (6-12)
  • Requires appointments over several weeks
  • Not all clinics offer treatment
  • Results variable depending on ED severity

Penile Injections (Alprostadil)

✓ Pros

  • Extremely reliable (90%+ success)
  • Works for most severe ED cases
  • Self-administered at home
  • Effects appear within 5-20 minutes

✗ Cons

  • Invasive needle injection required
  • Not spontaneous (requires planning)
  • Risk of priapism if overdosed
  • Ongoing cost and needle anxiety

Testosterone Therapy

✓ Pros

  • Addresses root cause if hypogonadal
  • Improves libido and energy levels
  • Multiple delivery options available
  • Relatively affordable long-term

✗ Cons

  • Only effective if testosterone deficient
  • Requires ongoing monitoring
  • Potential side effects (acne, gynecomastia)
  • Not effective for non-hormonal ED

Vacuum Erection Device (VED)

✓ Pros

  • Completely non-invasive and drug-free
  • Very low cost (one-time purchase)
  • Effective physical rehabilitation tool
  • No side effects or contraindications

✗ Cons

  • Requires learning curve and technique
  • Not spontaneous erections
  • Can be cumbersome during intimacy
  • Moderate effectiveness for severe ED

Penile Implant (Surgical)

✓ Pros

  • Permanent solution for severe ED
  • High satisfaction rates (90%+)
  • Spontaneous erections possible
  • No ongoing medication needed

✗ Cons

  • Surgical procedure with recovery time
  • High upfront cost (£10,000-£20,000)
  • Potential complications (infection, mechanical failure)
  • Irreversible decision

Which Treatment is Right for You?

Patient scenarios to help guide your decision

Scenario 1: Mild ED, First Time Treatment

Profile: Healthy man, no serious conditions, ED symptoms recent

Recommendation: Start with oral PDE5 inhibitors (Viagra/Cialis). Non-invasive, effective, and established safety. If unsatisfactory after 3 months or prefer drug-free approach, consider shockwave therapy.

Scenario 2: Moderate ED, Failed Medications

Profile: Tried oral meds without sufficient improvement, wants long-term solution

Recommendation: P-Shot + Shockwave combination in Turkey. Addresses vascular root cause, synergistic benefits, 12-24 month duration, significant cost savings.

Scenario 3: Severe ED, Refractory to All Treatments

Profile: Tried multiple treatments without success, seeking permanent solution

Recommendation: Consultation for penile implant with specialist urologist. 90%+ satisfaction, permanent results, but requires careful consideration and surgical recovery.

Scenario 4: Young Man (Under 40), Drug-Free Preference

Profile: Young, vascular ED, wants non-pharmaceutical approach

Recommendation: Shockwave therapy as first-line regenerative treatment. Drug-free, non-invasive, evidence-backed. Lifestyle modifications critical (exercise, smoking cessation).

Scenario 5: Post-Prostate Surgery, Nerve Damage

Profile: ED following prostatectomy, may involve nerve injury

Recommendation: P-Shot specialised protocol for post-surgical rehabilitation, VED for tissue conditioning, timeline 12-24 months. Consider injectable medications if rapid improvement needed.

Scenario 6: ED with Confirmed Low Testosterone

Profile: Blood tests confirm hypogonadism, hormonal ED suspected

Recommendation: Testosterone replacement therapy first with ongoing monitoring. Reassess ED symptoms at 6 months. If insufficient improvement, add P-Shot or shockwave therapy.
