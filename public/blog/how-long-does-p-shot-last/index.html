How Long Does the P-Shot Last? Complete 2025 Guide

By Dr. Sarah Thompson, Urologist — Medically Reviewed

The P-Shot (Priapus Shot) is a platelet-rich plasma (PRP) treatment that has become increasingly popular for men seeking a long-lasting, non-surgical solution to erectile dysfunction. One of the most common questions we receive is: how long does the P-Shot actually last?

Typical Duration of P-Shot Results

Most patients experience P-Shot results lasting between 12 and 18 months, with some men reporting meaningful benefits for up to 24 months after a single treatment. These figures are consistent across multiple clinical reviews and patient outcome studies.

The longevity of results depends on several interconnected factors:

  • Age and baseline vascular health — younger men with good cardiovascular function tend to experience longer-lasting results
  • Severity of erectile dysfunction — men with mild to moderate vascular ED typically respond better and for longer
  • Lifestyle habits — smoking, heavy alcohol use and poor diet accelerate vascular decline and shorten results
  • Quality of the PRP preparation — higher platelet concentration produces more growth factors and stronger tissue regeneration
  • Combination with shockwave therapy — patients who combine P-Shot with low-intensity shockwave therapy (Li-ESWT) consistently report extended duration

When to Expect Results

Results from the P-Shot are not immediate. The PRP triggers a biological regeneration process that unfolds over weeks:

  • Weeks 1–3: Initial tissue response; some men notice mild sensitivity changes
  • Weeks 4–8: Noticeable improvements in erection quality and firmness begin
  • 3–6 months: Full results typically realised, including improved spontaneous erections, enhanced sensitivity and increased confidence

Extending Your Results

Annual booster treatments are commonly recommended to maintain optimal results. Additional strategies include:

  • Combining with a course of low-intensity shockwave therapy (6–12 sessions)
  • Addressing cardiovascular risk factors: blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes management
  • Quitting smoking and moderating alcohol consumption
  • Regular aerobic exercise to maintain penile blood flow
  • Optimising testosterone levels if low

P-Shot Booster Treatments

A booster P-Shot can be administered as early as 3 months after the initial treatment if desired, though most patients wait until results begin to diminish — typically at 12–18 months. Many men who combine P-Shot with shockwave therapy find a booster is not needed until 24 months or beyond.

Cost Consideration

Given that P-Shot results typically last 12–18 months, the annual cost is considerably lower than ongoing oral medication. In Turkey, a P-Shot costs £800–£1,500 versus £2,500–£3,500 in the UK. When factoring in the longevity of results, treatment in Turkey provides exceptional value.
