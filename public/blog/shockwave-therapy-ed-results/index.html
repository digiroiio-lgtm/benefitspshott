Shockwave Therapy for ED: Real Results & What to Expect

By Dr. Sarah Thompson, Urologist — Medically Reviewed

Low-intensity shockwave therapy (Li-ESWT) has emerged as one of the most evidence-backed non-invasive treatments for erectile dysfunction. Multiple randomised controlled trials demonstrate 60–75% improvements in IIEF (International Index of Erectile Function) scores, making it a compelling option for men with vascular ED.

How Shockwave Therapy Works

Unlike oral medications that temporarily increase blood flow, shockwave therapy works by promoting neovascularisation — the formation of new blood vessels in penile tissue. Acoustic energy waves are applied to the penis via a handheld device, triggering:

  • Release of angiogenic growth factors (VEGF, FGF)
  • Activation of dormant stem cells in penile tissue
  • Disruption of micro-calcifications that restrict blood flow
  • Improved nerve conduction in penile tissue

This is a genuine tissue-level repair mechanism — not a symptomatic workaround.

What the Clinical Evidence Shows

A 2017 meta-analysis in the European Urology journal reviewed 14 RCTs involving 833 patients and found:

  • IIEF-EF domain improved by 6.8 points on average (clinically meaningful threshold is 4 points)
  • Erectile Hardness Score improved by 1.36 points
  • Minimal adverse events; no serious complications reported

A 2021 review by the Sexual Medicine Society of North America concluded that Li-ESWT is effective for mild to moderate vascular ED and can restore erectile function sufficient to allow sexual intercourse in many patients who had been non-responsive to PDE5 inhibitors.

What to Expect: Session by Session

  • Sessions 1–3: Treatment is painless and takes approximately 20 minutes. Most men experience no immediate changes; the biological repair process is beginning
  • Sessions 4–6: Some men begin noticing firmer morning erections and improved spontaneous arousal
  • 4–8 weeks post-treatment: Significant improvements in erection quality for the majority of responders
  • 3 months post-treatment: Peak results typically achieved; many men report erection quality they hadn't experienced in years

Standard Protocol

Most clinical protocols consist of 6–12 sessions, typically administered twice weekly over 3–6 weeks. Maintenance sessions may be recommended at 6–12 month intervals. Combining with the P-Shot in the same treatment package produces superior and longer-lasting results compared to either treatment alone.

Who Benefits Most?

Shockwave therapy is most effective for:

  • Men with mild to moderate vascular ED
  • Men who have partial response to oral medications
  • Men who have failed oral medications and want to avoid injections or implants
  • Men who want to restore spontaneous erections rather than rely on medication

It is less effective for severe vascular ED, neurological ED and hormonal ED without addressing the hormonal component.
