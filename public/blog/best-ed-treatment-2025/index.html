Best ED Treatment in 2025: Complete Expert Comparison Guide

By Dr. Robert Chen, Andrologist — Medically Reviewed

The landscape of erectile dysfunction treatment has transformed significantly in recent years. This guide evaluates seven treatment options across six clinically relevant criteria to help you make an informed decision.

The 7 ED Treatments Compared

  1. Oral PDE5 inhibitors (Viagra, Cialis, Levitra)
  2. P-Shot (Platelet-Rich Plasma therapy)
  3. Low-intensity shockwave therapy (Li-ESWT)
  4. Penile injections (alprostadil/trimix)
  5. Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT)
  6. Vacuum erection devices (VED)
  7. Penile implants (surgical)

Expert Verdict by ED Severity

Mild ED

Best choice: Oral PDE5 inhibitors or shockwave therapy

For mild, occasional ED, oral medications are convenient and affordable. Shockwave therapy is the preferred option for men who want a lasting solution without ongoing medication.

Moderate Vascular ED

Best choice: P-Shot + shockwave therapy combination

This combination addresses the vascular root cause from two complementary angles — PRP stimulates tissue regeneration while shockwave promotes neovascularisation. Clinical outcomes consistently outperform either treatment alone.

Severe Vascular ED (failed oral medications)

Best choice: Combined regenerative protocol, consider implant if unsuccessful

Men who have not responded to PDE5 inhibitors often achieve significant improvement with intensive regenerative protocols. Penile implants remain the last-resort option with the highest satisfaction rates.

Hormonal ED (low testosterone)

Best choice: TRT first, then reassess

Low testosterone should always be assessed and treated before other interventions. If TRT alone is insufficient, add regenerative treatments.
