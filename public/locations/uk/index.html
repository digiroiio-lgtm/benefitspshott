Private ED Treatment in the UK

The UK has a growing number of private clinics offering advanced ED treatments including P-Shot (PRP therapy) and low-intensity shockwave therapy. These treatments are not available on the NHS and must be accessed privately.

What's Available in the UK

  • P-Shot (PRP therapy) — available at specialist men's health and urology clinics
  • Low-intensity shockwave therapy — increasingly available at private urology clinics and men's health centres
  • Penile Doppler ultrasound — available at most private urology departments
  • Comprehensive hormonal assessment — available through private GPs and endocrinologists

Choosing a UK Clinic

When choosing a UK clinic for ED treatment, look for:

  • GMC-registered urologist or andrologist performing the procedure
  • CQC-registered facility
  • Transparent published pricing
  • Pre-treatment assessment including blood tests
  • Clear aftercare protocol
  • Genuine patient reviews on independent platforms (Google, Trustpilot)

NHS vs Private

The NHS does not provide P-Shot or shockwave therapy for ED. NHS ED treatment is limited to oral PDE5 inhibitors (on prescription), vacuum devices and, in severe cases, surgical implants. Men who want regenerative treatments must access them privately — either in the UK or internationally.
