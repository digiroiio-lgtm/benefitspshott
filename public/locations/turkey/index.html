Why Choose Turkey for ED Treatment?

Turkey has become the leading medical tourism destination in Europe for advanced ED treatments including P-Shot and shockwave therapy. The combination of highly trained specialists, modern infrastructure and significantly lower costs has attracted patients from across the UK, Europe, Middle East and North America.

Clinical Standards

  • JCI-accredited clinics — the gold standard in international healthcare accreditation
  • Urologists and andrologists trained at leading European and US institutions
  • Same medical-grade equipment as UK/US clinics (FDA-approved devices)
  • Rigorous pre-treatment assessment including hormonal screening and Doppler ultrasound
  • Full aftercare and follow-up via video consultation

How to Get There

Istanbul is served by direct flights from most UK airports (3 hours from London). Antalya and Izmir also have direct routes from UK hubs. Typical trip duration: 3–4 days (1 day travel, 1–2 treatment days, 1 day recovery/travel).

Is It Safe to Travel to Turkey for Medical Treatment?

Medical tourism to Turkey is well-established and well-regulated. The country's healthcare regulatory body (TÜRKIYE Sağlık Turizmi Koordinasyon Kurulu) oversees accredited providers. Patients should ensure they use JCI-accredited or Ministry of Health-registered facilities — our partner clinics all meet this standard.
